# Code of Conduct

Chera is built by and for people working under difficult network conditions.
We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their country, language,
background or experience.

## Our standards

Please:

- be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a
  second language;
- assume good faith, and ask before assuming;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- keep discussions about the project, its code and the networks it diagnoses.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- discriminatory language or content;
- publishing anyone's private information, including details that could put a
  person at risk in their country;
- asking for, or sharing, information intended to identify contributors or
  users;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

Because some contributors live in countries where discussing filtering can be
risky, please respect pseudonymity and never pressure anyone to reveal their
identity or location.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/assaabriiii/chera/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports will be handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may ban
people temporarily or permanently, for behaviour they consider harmful.

This code of conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
